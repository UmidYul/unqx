# UNQX Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

**Last updated:** 2026-03-28
**Effective date:** 2026-03-28

UNQX is a digital business card service (NFC/QR sharing, profile links, analytics) intended for adults (18+).
UNQX has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

This standard supplements:
- Terms of Service: https://unqx.uz/terms
- Privacy Policy: https://unqx.uz/policy

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all UNQX users, profile pages, links, account metadata, and behavior on UNQX website and mobile app.

## 2. Prohibited content and conduct
UNQX strictly prohibits:
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including known, suspected, or linked material.
- Any sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
- Grooming, solicitation, coercion, extortion, or enticement of minors.
- Human trafficking, including child trafficking, recruitment, facilitation, or promotion.
- Any use of UNQX to distribute, promote, request, or normalize CSAE-related content or activity.
- Attempts to evade enforcement actions, including repeat or mirror accounts.

## 3. How to report
You can report suspected violations through:
- Email: support@unqx.uz (subject: CSAE Report)
- Privacy/legal: privacy@unqx.uz
- Telegram: @unqx_uz
- Phone: +998338494444

Please include:
- Profile URL/slug or other identifier.
- Why you believe it violates this policy.
- Screenshots or other relevant evidence.
- Date, time, and timezone of observed activity.
- Your contact email for follow-up (optional for anonymous reports).

If there is immediate danger to a child, contact emergency services first, then notify UNQX.

## 4. Response times
- Initial acknowledgment target: within 1 business day.
- High-risk reports are prioritized immediately and may be restricted before full review.
- Investigation timelines vary by complexity, legal requirements, and evidence quality.

## 5. What happens after report
- Report registration and triage.
- Risk review of relevant profile/account content and technical signals.
- Temporary restriction when needed to reduce immediate risk.
- Evidence preservation where required by law.
- Final decision and enforcement action.

## 6. Moderation and enforcement
UNQX may apply one or more actions:
- Immediate removal or restriction of violating content.
- Temporary suspension or permanent account termination.
- Restriction of related accounts or identifiers where technically feasible.
- Escalation to competent law enforcement authorities where required or appropriate.

## 7. Emergency / law enforcement requests
Emergency and lawful requests can be sent to:
- privacy@unqx.uz
- support@unqx.uz

Requests should include agency identity, legal basis, scope, and urgency.
UNQX discloses data only as permitted or required by applicable law and UNQX policies.

## 8. Data handling for reports
To investigate and enforce this policy, UNQX may process:
- Reporter-provided information and evidence.
- Account/profile identifiers and associated logs.
- Technical metadata required for abuse prevention and legal compliance.

UNQX does not sell personal data.
Report data is handled with minimization, security, and retention controls consistent with legal obligations.

## 9. False reports policy
- Good-faith reports are encouraged.
- Knowingly false, malicious, or abusive reports may result in account-level enforcement.
- Repeated abuse of reporting channels may lead to access restrictions.

- Торговля людьми, включая торговлю детьми.
